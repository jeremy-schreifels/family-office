# Our Family Charter

## 1. Mission Statement
The purpose of the Micro Family Office is to manage the family's assets as a self-sustaining enterprise (the "Evergreen Model"). We use our resources to foster independence for family members, maintain a global perspective, and create a lasting, positive impact on our local and global ecology.

## 2. Core Values
* **Ecological Stewardship:** We prioritize environmental health in our investments, philanthropy, and lifestyle. We actively support environmental conservation and biodiversity.
* **Global Perspective:** We value cross-cultural experiences, international travel, and global relationships as essential tools for learning and personal growth.
* **Intentional Independence:** We view wealth as a tool to enable purposeful work, not as a replacement for it. We expect all family members to cultivate their own skills, careers, and financial competence.
* **Disciplined Execution:** We rely on data, structured processes, and expert counsel to make financial and operational decisions. We separate emotional bias from asset management.

## 3. Governance and Decision Rights
* **Portfolio CEO (John Smith):** Holds executive decision-making authority over the master balance sheet, asset allocation, and real estate operations.
* **Next-Generation Stewards (Abigail and Ben):** Actively participate in financial literacy education and manage their independent assets.
* **Annual Family Assembly:** The family holds a formal meeting once a year to review the master balance sheet, discuss the investment thesis, and allocate the annual philanthropic budget. 

## 4. Philanthropic Thesis
Our charitable giving and volunteer efforts focus largely on measurable environmental or societal impact. 
* **Primary Focus Areas:** Environmental conservation, climate change action, low-income housing assistance, and public broadcasting.
* **Giving Mechanism:** The family allocates a set percentage of the portfolio's yield annually to a Donor-Advised Fund (DAF). The Next-Generation Stewards hold voting rights on 50% of the DAF's annual grant distributions to practice philanthropic due diligence.

## 5. Next-Generation Stewardship
We do not use wealth to control the next generation. Instead, we provide structured education to prepare them for eventual co-management of the family enterprise.
* **Phase 1 (Current):** Understand basic budgeting, tax-advantaged accounts, and the mechanics of their own property/assets.
* **Phase 2 (Near-Term):** Participate in family assemblies, review the "Buy Box" criteria for new investments, and assist with philanthropic grant research.
* **Phase 3 (Long-Term):** Transition to active decision-making roles within the Management Company and Holding Company structures.
