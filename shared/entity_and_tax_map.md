# Entity and Tax Map

*(Note: This document tracks the legal architecture and tax compliance obligations for the Micro Family Office. Do not store full EINs or SSNs in this file.)*

## 1. Legal Entity Architecture

### The Vault (Asset Holding & Protection)
* **Smith Family Revocable Trust:**
  * **Structure:** Revocable Living Trust.
  * **Purpose:** Holds primary real estate, taxable brokerage accounts, and serves as the primary estate transfer vehicle to avoid probate.
  * **Grantor/Trustee:** John Smith.

## 2. Tax Jurisdictions & Residency

* **Primary Residency:** California, USA (Subject to federal and state income tax, plus California estate tax threshold considerations).

## 3. Cross-Border & Regulatory Compliance (CRITICAL)

* **FBAR (FinCEN Form 114):** Required annually if the aggregate value of foreign bank accounts (or any other foreign accounts) exceeds $10,000 USD at any point in the calendar year.
* **FATCA (Form 8938):** Required with the annual Form 1040 if foreign financial assets (including offshore accounts tied to the Beijing properties) exceed IRS filing thresholds.
* **Foreign Real Estate:** Direct ownership of foreign real estate is not subject to FBAR, but any rental income, capital gains upon sale, or foreign bank accounts used to maintain the property are fully reportable to the IRS.

## 4. Annual Tax Calendar & Milestones
* **January 15:** Q4 Estimated Tax Payment due.
* **March 15:** Partnership tax returns due.
* **April 15:** 
  * Q1 Estimated Tax Payment due.
  * Federal Form 1040 & California State Return due.
  * FBAR (FinCEN 114) deadline (automatic extension to October 15 available).
* **June 15:** Q2 Estimated Tax Payment due.
* **September 15:** Q3 Estimated Tax Payment due.
* **December 31:** Deadline for tax-loss harvesting, Roth conversions, and DAF charitable funding for the calendar year.
